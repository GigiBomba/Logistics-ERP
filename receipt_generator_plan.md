# Operion AI Prompt – Design & Implement a Professional Receipt Generator

## Objective

Design and implement a **Receipt Generator** inside the existing **Generators** workspace of Operion.

This generator should match the design language of the existing:

* CMR Generator
* Invoice Generator
* Proforma Invoice Generator

It must feel like a premium, enterprise-grade logistics ERP used by real transport companies.

The goal is to create a receipt generator that is **beautiful, extremely intuitive, legally compliant for EU businesses**, and capable of generating receipts for both customers and internal company operations.

---

# General Design Principles

The Receipt Generator should follow the exact visual identity of Operion.

The interface must be:

* clean
* modern
* spacious
* information-dense without feeling cluttered
* highly professional
* suitable for companies handling thousands of documents

Avoid looking like generic accounting software.

Avoid outdated Windows-looking controls.

Everything should align perfectly.

Maintain consistent spacing, margins, typography and iconography with the rest of the application.

Use subtle shadows, rounded corners, elegant section cards and proper visual hierarchy.

Every field should have sensible placeholder text.

Required fields should be clearly marked.

Validation errors should appear inline.

---

# Navigation

Inside the existing **Generators** tab, add:

* CMR
* Invoice
* Proforma Invoice
* **Receipt** ← New

The Receipt generator should instantly feel like it belongs there.

---

# Layout

Use a two-column layout.

Left side:

Document information.

Live receipt preview.

The preview should update in real time while editing.

The preview should look identical to the exported PDF.

# Overall Goal

The final result should look like software from a premium enterprise logistics suite rather than a basic accounting application.

It should be visually consistent with the rest of Operion, highly polished, easy to use, and capable of generating professional EU-compliant receipts suitable for logistics companies, transport services, customer payments, employee reimbursements, fuel expenses, toll expenses, and other business-related financial transactions.

Prioritize clarity, elegance, workflow efficiency, and seamless integration with the rest of Operion's generators and business data.
